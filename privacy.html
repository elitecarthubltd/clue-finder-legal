Clue Finder – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Clue Finder ("the game") is a puzzle mini game played inside the TikTok app. It is published by VSN Technologies Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you play.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you. The game has no accounts, no sign-up form and no servers of its own.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (which rooms you have unlocked, your star ratings and any free hints) is saved only on your device using the TikTok app's local storage. It is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the game's data in TikTok or by removing the mini game.

3. TikTok login

When the game starts it uses TikTok's silent login, as TikTok requires. This gives the game a temporary code. The game does not send this code anywhere, does not exchange it for your TikTok identifier and does not request your username, profile picture or any other profile data.

4. Advertising

The game shows ads provided by TikTok's In-App Ads service: optional rewarded video ads (for hints) and occasional full-screen ads between rooms. These ads are served and measured by TikTok, which may process information about your device and ad interactions under the TikTok Privacy Policy. We only receive aggregated reports (for example, total ad views and revenue), not information that identifies you.

5. Analytics

We do not run our own analytics. TikTok provides us with aggregated, non-identifying statistics about how many people play the game.

6. Children

The game is available only to people allowed to use TikTok under TikTok's own age rules. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the game collects no personal information from anyone.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by TikTok, see the TikTok Privacy Policy or contact TikTok.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date.
